It is not a carbon-credit registry, certification system, validation/verification service, legal opinion, accounting opinion, investment recommendation, tax advice, or assurance statement. Results are estimates generated from user inputs and illustrative assumptions. They must not be represented as verified emission reductions, issued Carbon Credit Certificates (CCCs), offsets, removals, Scope 1/2/3 inventory results, or guaranteed financial value.
Methodology disclosure: The calculator does not substitute for an approved methodology, project design document, baseline study, monitoring plan, additionality assessment, uncertainty assessment, leakage assessment, permanence assessment where applicable, validation, verification or registry decision. Reference: BEE, “Methodologies and Tools under Offset Mechanism”, last updated 7 July 2026.
Credit disclosure: A modeled tCO₂e reduction is not automatically a carbon credit. Under the CCTS Offset Mechanism, project registration and issuance are subject to applicable eligibility, methodology, monitoring and validation/verification requirements. This tool does not predict or guarantee issuance.
Reference: BEE CCTS Offset Mechanism materials and approved methodologies.
Baseline & factor disclosure: The baseline practice share, eligible-area share and credit-view haircut are screening inputs; actual baseline, additionality, leakage and uncertainty treatment must follow the applicable methodology. The crop-level reduction factors in this tool are illustrative screening placeholders for scenario analysis. They are not claimed to be official BEE/CCTS factors. For an actual project, replace them with methodology-specific equations, parameters, activity data and project/baseline calculations supported by appropriate evidence.
Ownership & double-counting disclosure: In contract-grower seed production, the right to claim or sell reductions depends on agreements between the company and growers. Reductions sold as credits generally cannot also be claimed by the company as insetting or Scope 3 reductions. This tool does not assess ownership or claim rights.
